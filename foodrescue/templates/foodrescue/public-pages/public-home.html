Find Food Near Me

How it works

1. Browse distribution venues near you

2. Go to the venue during the listed distribution time.

3. Collect the rescued food!

*Food availability may vary and is offered on a first-come, first-served basis.

How food gets to you safely

1. Establishments list their surplus food

Verified donors upload safe food items.

2. Organisations arrange pickup

Approved organisations reserve listings and collect them.

3. Food is prepared for redistribution

Organisations sort collected foods and assign them to community distribution venues.

4. Residents collect from public venues

You can check venue details online and visit collection points during the stated timing.

For your safety: All establishments and organisations are manually vetted and verified by admins!
